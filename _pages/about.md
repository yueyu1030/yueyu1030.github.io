Hello! I am a Research Scientist at the [FAIR CodeGen Team](https://ai.meta.com/research/), Meta.
My research interests lie at the intersection of large language models, reasoning, post-training, and data-centric AI.
My current work focuses on improving the agentic coding capabilities of Meta’s next-generation LLMs. More broadly, I have worked on a range of topics in LLM post-training, including code reasoning, reinforcement learning, and instruction following.

Prior to joining Meta, I completed my PhD at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://gatech.edu/) in December 2024. Before that, I received my bachelor's degree with honors from the Department of Electronic Engineering at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) in July 2019.
